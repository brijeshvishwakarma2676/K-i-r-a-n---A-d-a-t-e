# Video prompts (Google Flow)

## Memory (paste once in Agent settings → Memory)

```
This project makes short, realistic background clips for a sculptor's portfolio website.
- Photorealistic documentary footage only: natural colour, real physics, fine film grain, 24 fps. Never a CGI, 3D-render, fantasy or "cinematic AI" look.
- Never show a finished statue or sculpture, and never redraw, re-pose or change any artwork in an uploaded image.
- No faces, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no lens flares, no dramatic colour grading.
- Stone dust is heavy and falls quickly; it never rises or curls like smoke or steam.
- People: at most one person, shown only as hands and forearms, with exactly two hands.
- Slow, steady camera; no fast moves, no cuts. No camera equipment (sliders, rails, tripods, lights) anywhere in the frame.
```

## V1. The hand on the stone

```
A 15-second, single continuous documentary close-up in an open-air stone yard in India, late evening.

Subject: one old sculptor's right hand and forearm, dark weathered skin, thick calluses, short nails, fine white marble dust in the knuckle creases. The hand rests on the top of a freshly carved block of matte white marble. The top is covered in straight, parallel chisel grooves that all run in one direction, like combed lines; no grid, no squares, no rows of marks that look like writing.

Camera: a very slow, smooth move to the right at table height, 85mm lens, f/2.8, focus on the fingertips. No camera equipment anywhere in the frame; the block stands on the ground. The background is a soft golden blur of other stone blocks.

Light: low warm sun from the left, raking across the stone so each chisel groove casts a thin shadow.

Action:
[00:00-00:05] The hand rests flat and still; a few specks of dust glint.
[00:05-00:11] The open palm sweeps slowly across the surface; a small pinch of white dust slides off the far edge and drops straight down in a short, thin trickle that is gone within a second.
[00:11-00:15] The fingertips trace one chisel groove and stop.

Avoid: tools, a second hand, a face, a shirt sleeve, camera rails or equipment, a grid pattern on the stone, a long pouring stream of dust, smoke, floating dust, the stone denting like clay, lens flare, text.
```

## V2. Stainless steel

```
A 15-second, single continuous macro shot of polished stainless steel in a quiet workshop.

Subject: a long, gently curving band of mirror-polished stainless steel, about the width of a hand, running diagonally across the whole frame like one section of a large ribbon. It is a band, not a dome or sphere. Faint circular polishing marks show on its surface.

Camera: locked on a tripod, 100mm macro lens, f/5.6. Behind the steel, only a softly blurred plain wall.

Light: cool, dim workshop shade, and one band of warm late-afternoon daylight from an unseen window.

Action:
[00:00-00:04] The steel is in cool shadow; a warm glow appears at the left edge.
[00:04-00:11] The band of light slides slowly along the curve from left to right; the blurred window reflection stretches and bends with the metal.
[00:11-00:15] The light leaves at the right, and the steel returns to the same shadow as the first frame, so the clip loops.

Avoid: people, hands, sparks, rainbow chrome, a dome or ball shape, other metal objects, CGI look, text.
```

## V3. Stone

```
A 15-second, single continuous macro shot of a marble surface at sunrise.

Subject: the face of a large block of white marble, filling the frame. The left half is rough and freshly split, sparkling with small crystals; the right half is worked with even, parallel chisel grooves. A few grains of white grit lie in the grooves.

Camera: locked, with a very slow push-in of about 5 percent over the whole clip, 100mm macro lens, f/8, everything sharp.

Light: it starts dim and cool, then low warm sunlight sweeps in from the left.

Action:
[00:00-00:05] The stone is mostly in shadow; its crystals glint faintly.
[00:05-00:12] Sunlight moves across the block; the shadows in the grooves shorten and every ridge, grain and crystal becomes clear.
[00:12-00:15] Three or four grains of grit roll down one groove under gravity and stop.

The stone is hard and completely still.
Avoid: tools, hands, people, dust clouds, smoke, carved shapes, lens flare, text.
```

## V5. Mixed media

```
A 15-second, single continuous close-up of two metals meeting, as rain begins.

Subject: a flat plate of weathered corten steel with a deep orange-brown rust texture meets a strip of mirror-polished stainless steel along one clean, straight seam that runs diagonally across the frame.

Camera: locked, looking down at about 45 degrees, 85mm lens, f/5.6.

Light: soft overcast daylight; the polished steel reflects a pale grey sky.

Action:
[00:00-00:05] Everything is dry and still.
[00:05-00:12] The first raindrops fall: on the rust each drop leaves a small dark round spot that spreads slightly; on the polished steel the drops bead up and reflect the sky.
[00:12-00:15] The rust is now darker and wet; a few drops run slowly down the steel.

Real water physics, normal speed, no slow motion.
Avoid: people, welding, sparks, puddles flooding the frame, the seam bending, text.
```

## V7. Relief surface

```

Subject: a large panel of pale grey cast stone (GRC) filling the frame, with an abstract relief of uneven horizontal ridges and grooves, like layers of rock sediment. No figures, no symbols, no repeating pattern.

Camera: locked, straight on to the wall, 50mm lens, f/8, everything sharp.

Light: daylight that slowly swings from the front to the right side.

Action:
[00:00-00:05] Flat frontal light; the relief looks almost smooth.
[00:05-00:12] The light swings round to the right; long shadows slowly grow out of every ridge.
[00:12-00:15] In strong raking light the surface looks deep and carved; the light holds steady.

Only the shadows move; the wall stays fixed.
Avoid: faces or shapes appearing in the texture, people, plants, a building, text.
```

## V8. Water over stone

```
A 15-second, single continuous macro shot of water running over natural stone in a shaded garden.

Subject: a thin, clear sheet of water sliding down a dark, uneven, wet natural stone surface. It ripples over the bumps, catches the light in small glints, and drips from a stone lip into a dark pool at the bottom of the frame. A green fern leaf sits at the left edge.

Camera: locked, 100mm macro lens, f/5.6.

Light: soft overcast daylight in shade, gentle highlights on the wet stone.

Action:
[00:00-00:15] The water flows steadily from the first frame to the last, so the clip loops. Around the middle, the fern leaf stirs once in a light breeze and settles.

Real water physics at normal speed, no slow motion. The stone never moves.
Avoid: a whole fountain, a garden view, people, splashing, text.
```

## H1. Dust in light (home page: behind the artist's words)

```
A 15-second, single continuous locked shot inside a dark stone-carving workshop, early morning.

Subject: one shaft of sunlight falls from a high, unseen window diagonally down through the dark room, from the upper right towards the lower centre. Very fine marble dust, like motes, hangs in the beam and drifts slowly down, glinting where the light is brightest. Outside the beam the room is near-black; only a rough stone wall behind is faintly visible.

Composition: the beam crosses the right two-thirds of the frame; the left third stays dark and empty, because words will sit there. The middle of the frame must also work cropped to a tall portrait.

Camera: locked, 50mm lens, f/4, focus on the dust in the middle of the beam.

Light: one warm, soft-edged beam and nothing else.

Action:
[00:00-00:05] The beam is steady; specks of dust drift down through it and sparkle as they cross its brightest part.
[00:05-00:12] A faint, slow draught moves the dust a little to one side, then it settles back to drifting straight down.
[00:12-00:15] The scene looks exactly as it did at the start, so the clip loops.

Avoid: people, tools, sculptures, smoke, steam, fog, dust that rises or swirls, a beam that pulses or flickers, lens flare, text.
```

## H4. The standing block (home page: the arched niche)

```

Composition: make it in 9:16 portrait if the option is there. Otherwise, centre the block exactly, inside the middle third of the frame with even space on both sides, so it can be cropped to a tall portrait.

Camera: locked, 85mm lens, f/4, the block sharp from top to bottom.

Light: cool, even light before sunrise, then the first warm sun.

Action:
[00:00-00:05] Cool, even pre-dawn light; the block stands still.
[00:05-00:12] A line of warm sunlight appears at the top edge and slides slowly down the face to the base, bringing out the veins and the split texture.
[00:12-00:15] The whole block glows warm and the light holds.

Avoid: carved forms, faces or figures appearing in the stone, people, tools, birds, the block changing shape, lens flare, text.
```
